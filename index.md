## Selected Work

### OpenUSD Reactive Simulation-On-Demand
Traditional physics simulations often cause computational bottlenecks in active viewports. I engineered a Python-based OpenUSD pipeline that solves this by detecting geometry collisions (using AABB bounding boxes) and handing the heavy computation over to a headless background process (hython). The simulation is procedurally generated, baked, and mapped back into the master scene graph only when needed.

### Computational Geometry & Algorithmic Design
I translate complex academic research into production-ready Houdini VEX implementations. My custom solutions leverage native data structures to build k-d trees, implement spatial hashing, and solve Laplacian meshes.

### Applied Machine Learning
From engineering threshold-based anomaly detection updates for enterprise risk systems to drafting formal IEEE research on Random Forest classifiers, I build data architectures that are precise, scalable, and rigorously evaluated.
